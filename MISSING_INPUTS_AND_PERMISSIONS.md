# Missing inputs, held files and open permissions

## A. Inputs not included (cannot be supplied)
1. **Study-area (AEZ-26) polygon.** A private Earth Engine asset. Its origin is stated (BARC Major Agro-Ecological Zone shapefile, extracted in QGIS Desktop 3.44.12; manuscript Section 2.1); the BARC edition is not recorded and the right to redistribute has not been verified (see `metadata/FIG1_PROVENANCE.md` in the software release). Without it Figure 1 and all Level-0 extractions cannot be regenerated by others.
2. **Bangladesh outline** (`BGD_adm0`, DIVA-GIS/GADM): not redistributable without permission.
3. **Source extracts and merge workflow for Phase 2A.** The Phase 2A notebook merges CHIRPS, ERA5-Land and GLDAS daily extracts into the 9,497-day backbone. The merge of chunked exports into the single daily files has no supplied code, the ERA5-Land and GLDAS daily files that were supplied are not the files the notebook names, and the backbone is held (section B). Phase 2A therefore cannot be reproduced from this release.
4. **Source inputs for Phases 2E and 2F.** The MODIS, GRACE and JRC quality-control files named by the notebooks (`AEZ26_MODIS_ET_PET_QC_2000_2025.csv`, `AEZ26_MODIS_NDVI_EVI_QC_2000_2025.csv`, `AEZ26_GRACE_Mascon_TWSA_Uncertainty.csv`, `AEZ26_JRC_SurfaceWater_Monthly_QC_2000_2021.csv`) and the Phase 2E link tables were not available or are not released. The Phase 2E and 2F notebooks are included for documentation but were not re-run, and Phase 2F outputs are released as the author-run results. **Phases 2A, 2E and 2F cannot be fully reproduced from this release.**
5. **Per-pixel and daily raw ERA5-Land inputs for Eq. 7 (1981–2025, 45 yearly exports plus the pixel-weights table).** Exported on 2026-11-15 and used for the offline recomputation, but **not redistributed** in this release: they are per-pixel ERA5-Land values whose redistribution terms have not been confirmed from the primary Copernicus documents, and the weights table reflects the private zone footprint. Names, row counts and SHA-256 are in `data/eq6_eq7_verification/eq7_fullrecord/pixel_export_sha256.csv`; the Earth Engine script that produced them is `gee/AEZ26_Eq7_FullRecord_SourceExport_v01.js`. No per-pixel soil-water layer exports exist, so Eq. 6 has no pixel-level check.
6. **Earth Engine Console output and `system:version` image properties** of the 23-pentad diagnostic and of the 2026-11-15 exports (weights, 2000, two batches, 12-pentad diagnostic) were not archived; the exported CSVs, their SHA-256 values and the scripts are included or recorded. The 2000 re-export is byte-identical to the earlier 2000 pilot export (documented in `EQ6_EQ7_VERIFICATION.md`; no effect on values).
7. **Access dates and download URLs** of each source product are not recorded (`metadata/source_datasets.csv`).
8. **Two historical Phase 2C catalogue copies** (40 rows, SHA-256 `69c8dc0cb664b2dd79f9311e2db51718a614fa3069269dc692dd05f89e929292`; 45 rows, `db6e218ba075c81fc2802d65563537e14cb19fbc7d1f3ec300b0a0cfcffa389a`) listed in the stored Phase 7A Candidate Audit were not available. The audit can be reproduced only for the 39-row copy; the limitation is described in `documentation/PHASE7A_INPUT_PROVENANCE.md`.
9. **A pentad-table version with a hash beginning `d397`** reported by the author was not found and was not compared.

## C. Provider terms
Terms were located for all seven products (`metadata/source_datasets.csv`). For ERA5-Land the terms are a Copernicus licence agreement that was read only as summarised on the Earth Engine catalogue page; for GLDAS-2.1 the page states that data are "available free to the user community" and refers to a NASA policy that was not read. **The author should confirm both against the primary documents before deposit.** The derived files contain regional aggregates and event metrics, not the gridded source products.
