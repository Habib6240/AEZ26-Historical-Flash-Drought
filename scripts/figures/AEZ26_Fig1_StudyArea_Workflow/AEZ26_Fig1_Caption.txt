Fig. 1. Study area and analytical workflow. (a) The High Barind Tract (Agro-Ecological Zone 26, AEZ-26), Bangladesh: study boundary (≈ 1,545.55 km²) in WGS 84 / UTM zone 45N with a straight 10 km UTM grid (easting and northing in km; geographic coordinates are marked by ticks on the top and right edges), a scale bar whose length is corrected for the point-scale factor, a grid-north arrow and an inset locating AEZ-26 within a generalized outline of Bangladesh (BGD_adm0 country outline from DIVA-GIS, whose country data derive from GADM; edition not recorded; GADM permits use in published academic maps but not redistribution). The inset is a regional locator only and not a flash-drought footprint. The boundary is the AEZ-26 polygon from the BARC Major Agro-Ecological Zone shapefile that was used in Google Earth Engine for all series (manuscript Section 2.1); the plotted coordinates agree with the Earth Engine export of that polygon to within 10⁻⁸ degrees, and the area is the Earth Engine value. (b) Workflow: Column A: daily CHIRPS, ERA5-Land and GLDAS backbone, 73-pentad processing, GLDAS soil-moisture percentile detection, the 39-event catalogue, driver characterization, ERA5-Land source and record-length sensitivity, chronology agreement with a year-shift benchmark, and consensus-event sensitivity. Column B: native-time MOD16, MOD13, GRACE and JRC responses, response metrics, association tests with Benjamini–Hochberg control, the seasonal-control audit and independent reproduction of the tests.
