Figure 4. Land–water responses to the 39 primary flash-drought events and multiplicity-aware event-level inference. Responses are analysed in each product's native time for MOD16 ET/PET, MOD13 NDVI, JRC surface-water extent and GRACE Terrestrial Water Storage Anomaly (TWSA); valid event numbers differ among products. (a) Product-specific response depth (z_base − z_min; pre-event baseline z minus minimum z) for each valid event, with the interquartile range and median; negative depths are retained, and depths are not comparable across products as a measure of drought severity. (b) Lag from event onset to the response minimum (days) for each valid event; lags are bounded by each product's native temporal support (composites or monthly records) and do not resolve sub-composite timing, and no causal sequence from fast to slow responses is inferred. (c) Benjamini–Hochberg q-values for all 46 source-backed association tests in five families (primary drivers, secondary atmospheric variables, event physics, driver-class omnibus and cross-response coupling; q-values adjusted within family). Each family contains outcomes with different sample support, and the dashed line marks q = 0.05. No test satisfied q ≤ 0.05, so no FDR-supported association was detected; the two cross-response pairs with nominal p < 0.05 (MOD16–NDVI and TWSA–JRC; q = 0.0576) did not meet the FDR criterion. (d) Number of events with seasonal-normal recovery observed versus not observed within the finite observation window (right-censored); right-censoring does not establish permanent non-recovery, and recovery counts depend on the recovery definition (pre-baseline recovery is reported separately in the Supplement). GRACE TWSA is a storage anomaly, not groundwater, and JRC surface-water extent is not a storage measure. Some GRACE and JRC response minima are confounded and are retained as such rather than treated as isolated responses.
