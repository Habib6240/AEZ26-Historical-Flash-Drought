Figure 3. Source and record-length robustness of regional flash-drought detection. The GLDAS primary catalogue (39 events, 2000–2025) is compared with two ERA5-Land soil-moisture sensitivity inventories: OVERLAP26 (44 events, 2000–2025) and LONG45 (74 events, 1981–2025; 43 with onsets in 2000–2025). These are sensitivity analyses, not validation of either source. (a) Inventory sizes; counts for the common 2000–2025 period are separated from the extended 1981–2025 record, which is not directly comparable. (b) Cumulative one-to-one onset agreement with the GLDAS primary catalogue: number of the 39 GLDAS onsets matched within the exact pentad, ±1 pentad and ±2 pentads. Agreement increases with tolerance, but exact chronology remains source- and record-length-sensitive. (c) Onset-season shares (MAM, JJAS, ON, DJF) for the GLDAS primary catalogue (n = 39) and ERA5-Land LONG45 (n = 74); shares rather than counts are shown because record lengths differ. The JJAS concentration is comparatively preserved, although shares are not identical. (d) Event rates in three 15-year periods of ERA5-Land LONG45 (25, 25 and 24 events), with a summary of the long-record evidence: the annual event count shows no FDR-supported increase over 1981–2025, no tested long-record hydroclimate trend survives FDR correction, and continuous-state agreement between ERA5-Land and GLDAS is moderate (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.58 for soil-moisture percentiles and standardized anomalies), which does not imply event-date agreement. The absence of an FDR-supported historical frequency trend is not evidence of no climate change.
