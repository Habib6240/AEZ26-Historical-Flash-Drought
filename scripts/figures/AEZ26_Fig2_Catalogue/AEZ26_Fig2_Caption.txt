Figure 2. Primary regional flash-drought catalogue and event characteristics, High Barind Tract (AEZ-26), 2000–2025. (a) Chronology of the 39 AEZ-wide primary flash-drought events on a continuous calendar axis; each event is drawn from its onset (dot) to its event end (line) and placed in the row of its onset month. Light shading marks the June–September rows. The four prolonged events are labelled by onset pentad (year_pentad). (b) Number of primary events by season of onset (MAM, JJAS, ON, DJF); percentages are shares of the 39 events. Counts are descriptive and are not a formal test of seasonality. (c) Total event duration (pentads) against event severity (percentile-point pentads; logarithmic axis, which keeps the majority of events readable alongside the long severe tail), one point per event, coloured and shaped by primary forcing class (compound, precipitation-only, high-PE-only, neither). Dashed lines mark the median duration and median severity. A few exactly coincident points are offset horizontally by less than one pentad for visibility only. Forcing class describes conditions coincident with the rapid-intensification interval; it is descriptive, not causal attribution, and "neither" means that neither primary large-anomaly threshold was crossed, not that atmospheric forcing was absent. Events are region-wide series and do not represent spatial drought footprints.
