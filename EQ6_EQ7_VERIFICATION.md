## Equation 6 — formula-level reproduction PASS; source-level comparison for 35 pentads
SM (mm) = 70·θ1 + 210·θ2 + 720·θ3, ERA5-Land layers 0–7, 7–28 and 28–100 cm (volumetric water content, m³ m⁻³; coefficients are layer thicknesses in mm; 1,000 mm in total).
- Inputs: nine author-run Earth Engine exports of regional-mean layer values (`data/eq6_eq7_verification/eq6/layer_chunks/`), unchanged as received; SHA-256 in `eq6_chunk_sha256.csv`.
- Script `scripts/eq6_eq7_audit/eq6_reconstruction_audit.py` (written for this audit). Result: 3,285 of 3,285 pentads reproduced; max abs difference 4.55 × 10⁻¹³ mm. Re-applying wrong thicknesses (equal thirds, reversed, 100/100/800) fails by 13 to 131 mm (maximum absolute difference), so the check discriminates.
- Layer depths follow the ERA5-Land layer definitions (Muñoz-Sabater et al., 2021; Copernicus documentation lists layers 1–3 as 0–7, 7–28 and 28–100 cm). They were not re-queried in the Earth Engine catalogue (the catalogue page was not reachable in the audit environment).
- Only regional layer means were archived. The equation is linear, so weighting regional means equals weighting each pixel provided masks and weights are identical across the three layers; this was **not verified** (the diagnostic coverage columns use the temperature mask). Per-pixel layer values were not exported.
- Source-level comparison: `data/eq6_eq7_verification/eq7_fullrecord/AEZ26_Eq7_ABC_35pentads_with_offline.csv` (soil-water columns) and `scripts/eq6_eq7_audit/eq6_35pentad_soilwater_offsets.py` → `data/eq6_eq7_verification/eq6/eq6_35pentad_soilwater_offsets.csv`. B − A: all 35: mean +0.097 mm, mean abs 0.844 mm, max abs 3.248 mm (0.247 of the interannual SD). By state of the stored series: ≤ 20th percentile (n = 9) mean abs 0.68 mm, max 2.99 mm (0.061 SD); 20th–80th (n = 15) 0.81 / 2.51 mm (0.079 SD); ≥ 80th (n = 11) 1.03 / 3.25 mm (0.247 SD). All twelve months are represented (one to six pentads each; May, August and October thinly). The year-to-year SD of the offset (0.56 mm, used for the stress tests) comes from pentads 12 and 13 only (8 pentad-years).
- Consequence for the manuscript: the ERA5-Land soil-water series feed only the OVERLAP26 and LONG45 sensitivity catalogues (not the primary 39-event GLDAS catalogue). Table S20 shows that catalogue size and GLDAS agreement stay within the stated ranges for independent noise up to the largest observed offset (3.25 mm), whereas individual onset dates do not. A persistent offset cancels from the rank-based percentiles. Noise-stress extensions run during the 2026-11-15 Eq. 6 audit (annual-count trend, consensus set, higher noise levels) were executed in scratch only, are not part of this release, and the manuscript does not rely on them.
- The regional mean of the stored table used the archived reduction (below), so Eq. 6 inherits the same spatial-reduction caveat as Eq. 7.

## Equation 7 — numerical recomputation for 3,285 pentads; source-level comparison for 35
Daily per-pixel saturation and actual vapour pressure, es = 0.6108·exp[17.27·T/(T+237.3)] (T in °C) and ea (same expression at the dew point); RH = 100·ea/es limited to 0–100 %; VPD = max(es − ea, 0) kPa; averaged per pixel over the pentad (VPD maximum per pixel), then over the zone. The primary hourly VPD (Phase 1B) uses the constant 0.61078 kPa and does not use Eq. 7.
- **Pathways.** A: archived composite reduction (reduceRegion at 11,132 m on a temporal composite, which reports a 1° default nominal scale, 111,319 m, against 11,131.95 m native); B: explicit native grid; C: reproject to native, then reduce. 35 pentads: 23 (`data/eq6_eq7_verification/eq7/AEZ26_Eq7_Temporal_Robustness_23pentads_v02.csv`, script `gee/AEZ26_Eq7_Temporal_Robustness_DIAGNOSTIC_v02.js`) plus 12 (`eq7_fullrecord/AEZ26_Eq7FR_ABC_12pentads.csv`, script `gee/AEZ26_Eq7_FullRecord_SourceExport_v01.js`, mode DIAG). The 35 pentads come from 26 years (1982–2025), cover every month, and are a selected sample.
- **Full-record recomputation.** Per-pixel daily 2 m temperature and dew point of the 31 ERA5-Land pixels with a footprint weight, 1981-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (16,436 days; 509,516 pixel-day records), exported with the same script (modes WEIGHTS and PIXELS) and recomputed offline (`scripts/eq6_eq7_audit/eq7_fullrecord_recompute.py` → `eq7_fullrecord/AEZ26_Eq7_Recomputed_3285pentads.csv`). Integrity checks on the exports: 44 yearly files match the SHA-256 lists supplied with the two batches (`eq7_fullrecord/pixel_export_sha256.csv`); every year has the expected rows; 0 nulls; no dew point above temperature; weights imply 1,545.53 km² (sample) and 1,545.55 km² (native cells) against 1,545.55 km² expected. The exports themselves are **withheld** (see `MISSING_INPUTS_AND_PERMISSIONS.md`).
- **Agreement (stored − recomputed, native-cell weights, 3,285 pentads; `scripts/eq6_eq7_audit/eq7_fullrecord_checks.py`).** T bias −0.039 °C, MAE 0.047 °C, max 0.335 °C, r = 0.99993; RH bias +0.017 pp, MAE 0.21 pp, max 1.66 pp; VPD (mean) bias −0.0040 kPa, MAE 0.0088 kPa, max 0.103 kPa. Recomputation − pathway B in the 35 pentads: T max 0.0046 °C, RH max 0.040 pp, VPD max 0.0012 kPa.
- **Offset structure.** Systematic and seasonal (monthly means up to −0.11 °C and −0.022 kPa in May), similar in 1981–1999 and 2000–2025; the year-to-year SD of the offset for the same pentad is 0.038 °C, 0.21 pp and 0.0094 kPa (45 years). No step at 2000 (level shift −0.0084 °C, 95 % CI −0.0173 to +0.0005; VPD −0.0013 kPa, −0.0041 to +0.0015) and no drift (Theil–Sen per decade: T +0.0006 °C, −0.0012 to +0.0027; VPD +0.00003 kPa, −0.0005 to +0.0006).
- **Trend conclusions.** The recomputed series gives temperature +0.0904 °C per decade (95 % CI +0.0039 to +0.1797; p = 0.040) and VPD −0.0251 kPa per decade (−0.0487 to +0.0002; p = 0.050); with the manuscript's five-test Benjamini–Hochberg family the q-values for temperature, VPD and PE are 0.084 (stored series: 0.0879) and none reaches 0.05. The VPD interval reaches zero in both series; the margin is thin.
- **Demonstrated:** the archived pathway reproduces the stored values (35 pentads); the stored series differs from the per-pixel recomputation by a quantified, seasonal offset without step or drift; trend conclusions are unchanged. **Inferred:** a half-cell grid displacement as the cause (not read from Earth Engine); independent noise as a model of year-to-year variation. **Not established:** the hourly-versus-daily VPD difference before 2000; independence from the Tetens constants and daily-mean convention (all comparisons use the same Eq. 7 definition); the soil-water offset outside the 35 pentads.

## Provenance details of the 2026-11-15 exports (documentation only)
- The 2000 re-export (`AEZ26_Eq7FR_Pixel_T_Td_2000.csv`, SHA-256 `4a47ff88e461c21bab0090e0230337a682f0ec44777fdc90e1ebfc1dd69bbdbb`) is byte-identical to the earlier pilot export of the same year. Earth Engine exports of identical inputs are expected to be identical, so this is not evidence of an error; it also does not show an independent run. The scientific values are unaffected.
- The Earth Engine Console output and the `system:version` properties of the ERA5-Land images were not archived for these exports. Indirect evidence that the current collection matches the data behind the stored series: pathway A reproduced the stored values to ≤ 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁴ in the 35 pentads (35 of 3,285 pentads).
- The copy of `AEZ26_Eq7_FullRecord_SourceExport_v01.js` released here has a corrected header (it said "PROPOSED - NOT YET RUN"); the code lines are unchanged. The file hashes of the exported CSVs, not the Code Editor copy actually run, are the record of what was executed.
- Stored indicators were not modified, no correction factor was fitted, and the offline Python checks are not Earth Engine outputs. The stored series cannot be regenerated without Earth Engine and the private zone asset.
